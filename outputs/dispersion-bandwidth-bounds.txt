A. 400 random passive media (lossless oscillators incl. Drude, absorption bands, conducting band),
   evaluated in their transparency windows:
   min [d(w eps)/dw - eps0]              = +1.586e-03  (>= 0: [eq:derivative_bounds], l.814)
   min [w eps' - 2 (eps0 - eps)]          = -4.547e-13  (>= 0: sharper L&L causality bound)
   min w eps'/(eps0 - eps) over eps < eps0 = 2.000000  (l.814 claims >= 1; the true floor is 2)
   lossless Drude: |w eps' - 2(eps0 - eps)| = 8.9e-16  (equality)
