C. Andersen inequality over 4000 random lossy spheres (x = 0.05-15)
   length unit             max printed LHS/RHS  min printed  max corrected  max sabs/(l^2 D/4pi)
   lambda                           1.0000e+00   2.6776e-04     1.00000000              0.999861
   m (lambda = 1 um)                1.0000e-06   2.6776e-10     1.00000000              0.999861
   mm (lambda = 1 um)               1.0000e-03   2.6776e-07     1.00000000              0.999861
   nm (lambda = 1 um)               1.0000e+03   2.6776e-01     1.00000000              0.999861
   corrected form: equality when S(0) is real; best case |arg S(0)| = 8.23e-06, ratio 1.00000000 (m = 2.315+0.004j, x = 6.497)
   -> printed form: LHS/RHS scales as (length unit)^1, so it is dimensionally inconsistent;
      the optical-theorem form sigma_abs + sigma_sca <= lambda sqrt(sigma_sca D_s/pi) holds for all spheres.

   Example: 30-nm-radius sphere, eps = -2.2 + 0.3i, lambda = 500 nm: sigma_ext = 31357 nm^2, sigma_sca = 8908 nm^2, D_s = 1.503

      corrected RHS lambda sqrt(sigma_sca D/pi) = 32644 nm^2 >= sigma_ext = 31357 nm^2
